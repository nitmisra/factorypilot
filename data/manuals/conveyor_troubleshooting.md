# Sample Conveyor Troubleshooting Guide

> Portfolio demo content only. This is not an equipment manufacturer's manual and does not define safe operating limits.

## Motor temperature

If motor temperature trends upward, verify that the sensor reading is plausible and compare the trend with the equipment's approved operating envelope. A qualified technician may inspect cooling airflow, load conditions, and the maintenance history under the site's approved procedure. Do not touch or open equipment while it is energized.

## Abnormal vibration

If vibration rises above the configured monitoring threshold, check the sensor and review recent maintenance records. Under the approved lockout/tagout and inspection procedure, qualified personnel may inspect mounting, alignment, looseness, and bearing condition. Do not infer a confirmed mechanical failure from vibration alone.

## Conveyor speed or throughput drop

Compare commanded speed with measured speed and review upstream/downstream line states. Potential explanations include a drive/control issue, product accumulation, mechanical resistance, or a counting sensor issue. Follow site procedures before clearing a blockage or inspecting moving machinery.

## General incident handling

Record the time the issue began, relevant sensor trends, production impact, and actions taken. Escalate according to site policy when readings cross approved limits, multiple signals deteriorate, or the cause remains uncertain. Any intervention on physical equipment must be performed by authorized personnel.
