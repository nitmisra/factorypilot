# Sample Incident Walkthrough

1. Select **Motor overheating** in the sidebar.
2. Review the temperature trend and the detected threshold finding.
3. Click **Investigate incident**.
4. Review the possible explanations and the retrieved troubleshooting guidance.
5. Discuss what additional evidence would distinguish between sensor error, cooling issue, and increased mechanical load.
6. Emphasize that the demo suggests checks; it does not confirm a physical fault or control equipment.
